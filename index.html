StudySpace

A clickable prototype of our study room finder for Metro State. Rooms and schedules are sample data.

  • Search, or tap a vibe like Quiet, to narrow the rooms
  • Tap the heart on a room to save it
  • Pick an open time and book with a Star ID such as ab1234cd
  • See or cancel bookings under Profile
